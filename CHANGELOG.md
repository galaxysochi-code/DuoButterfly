## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-13

First public release.

### Added

- Lid-driven desktop effect for the built-in MacBook display with three styles:
  Silk, Dusk, and Mist.
- Adjustable bend, blur, darkness, and cutoff angle, with a live preview and a five-second demo.
- One window with Effect, Settings, and About sections; ON/OFF switch and the ⌘⌥B hotkey.
- Attention banner with a single next step for pause, missing screen access, capture errors,
  and an unavailable lid sensor.
- Interface in Russian, English, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish, switchable in Settings.
- Sound preview, launch at login, and a copyable diagnostics report.
- Menu bar icon that signals when the effect needs attention.
- "Say Thanks" banner and sheet with crypto wallet addresses, copy buttons,
  and payment QR codes generated on the Mac.

### Privacy

- No network access, accounts, or analytics.
