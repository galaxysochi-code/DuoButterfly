DUO Butterfly

Close the lid.Your desktop follows.

DUO Butterfly is a free menu bar app for MacBook. As you lower the lid, your desktop bends, blurs and dims.
Open it again, and everything is right where you left it.

How it works

The lid moves.The effect keeps pace.

DUO Butterfly reads your MacBook’s built-in lid-angle sensor. The lower the lid, the stronger the effect. Pause halfway, and the image pauses with you.

Privacy

Your screennever leavesyour Mac.

The effect needs screen recording permission. Frames are processed in memory with Metal and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app makes no network requests at all.

No account.No analytics.No audio recording.

Installation

Three steps.
About a minute.

Detailed installation guide ↗
  1. Download the ZIP file from the latest GitHub release, unzip it and drag DUO Butterfly to your Applications folder.
  2. The app isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Allow screen recording when prompted. It’s used only for the built-in display, and no audio is recorded.
